# BBB AI Gaming Platform

## Identity

BBB AI Gaming is a locally hosted AI-enhanced gaming infrastructure platform.

Built on:
- EPYC infrastructure
- RTX 6000 Blackwell
- Virtualized gaming systems
- Local LLMs
- AI orchestration
- Creator automation

## Features

### AI Spectator
Understands gameplay visually.

### AI Voice
Narrates and reacts to gameplay.

### Highlight Engine
Detects combat and exciting moments.

### Content Pipeline
Generates:
- stream titles
- summaries
- content packs
- social captions

### Dashboards
Web interfaces for:
- highlights
- reports
- summaries
- monitoring

### Infrastructure
- VM orchestration
- GPU monitoring
- backup systems
- operational tooling

## Philosophy

Local-first AI infrastructure.

No cloud dependency required.

## Long-Term Goal

Create premium AI-enhanced gaming and creator environments powered by BBB infrastructure.
